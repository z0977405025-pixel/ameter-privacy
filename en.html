1. Information We Collect

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") explains how Brown ("we," "us") collects, processes, uses, and protects your personal data when providing the iOS app 捷錶 (AMeter; Bundle ID: tw.brown.fare; the "App"). This Policy is provided in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act of the Republic of China (Taiwan). The App is a driver-use fare estimation and trip record tool. It is not a taxi meter and does not provide ride-hailing or dispatch. Limits on use are set out in the AMeter Terms of Service. The operator is Brown.

  1. Account data (provided by the sign-in platform you authorise, when you choose to sign in):
    • Sign in with Apple: the user identifier provided by Apple, and the name and email address you agree to share on first sign-in. You may use Apple's "Hide My Email," in which case we receive only the relay address generated by Apple.
    • Google sign-in: Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile-photo URL.
    • LINE sign-in: LINE user identifier, display name, and profile-photo URL.
    • We never receive your Apple, Google, or LINE password.
  2. Trip and rate settings:
    • Trip records (route tracks, date, start and end times, duration, distance, the rate used, and the estimated amount) are created and stored on your device by the App. We cannot read them.
    • Account settings and custom rates are stored in Google Cloud Firestore so they can be used and restored across devices. Location data, route tracks, and trip records are not included.
  3. GPS location (provided by your iPhone location services, with your authorisation):
    • We collect the device's location coordinates, timestamps, and speed only from the time you tap "Start trip" until you end the trip, in order to calculate distance and time, estimate a fare using the rate you selected, and draw a route sketch.
    • During a trip, the App continues to obtain location even if you switch to another app or lock the screen. Collection stops when the trip ends.
    • Location data is stored on your device and is not sent to us. If we later offer cloud backup or sync, we will inform you and obtain your consent before turning it on.
  4. Map screenshots you upload: only when you actively use "AI screenshot fare estimate," the map screenshot you select and upload, and the recognition and estimate returned by the AI service. A screenshot may include a route, origin and destination, place names or addresses, date and time, distance and duration, and the phone status bar or notification content.
  5. Other data we may collect:
    • Usage data (Google Analytics for Firebase, with your consent): install identifier, usage activity, and general device and OS information. We do not send GPS coordinates, route tracks, screenshot content, or any passenger-related data to the analytics service.
    • Crash and diagnostic data (Firebase Crashlytics): crash time, app and OS versions, device model, stack traces, and similar information.
    • Purchase and subscription data (provided by the Apple App Store): transaction identifier, plan, purchase and expiry times, and renewal and refund status. Payment is handled by Apple. We do not receive your full credit-card number.
    • The email address, message content, and attachments you send when you write to support.
  6. We do not ask you to enter a passenger's name, phone number, or other contact details. We do not access your contacts, microphone, or camera, and the App does not show ads. We do not collect sensitive personal data specified in Article 6 of the Personal Data Protection Act.

2. How We Use Your Information

  1. We collect the data above in order to provide fare estimation and trip-record features (estimating a fare from location and the selected rate), to sync or restore rate settings, to manage your account, and to respond to support requests. The fare shown on screen is only an estimate. It is not a receipt or invoice and cannot replace the certified meter in the vehicle.
  2. If you consent to "AI screenshot fare estimate," we will send the screenshot you upload to a third-party AI service to recognise the route, distance, and time and to estimate an amount.
  3. If a paid plan is introduced in the future, we will use purchase and subscription data to confirm your entitlements. If paid features are introduced, prices and terms will be announced separately.
  4. With your consent, we may use de-identified or aggregated usage data to improve the interface and stability, and we may use crash data to find and fix errors and to prevent abuse.
  5. We will use your personal data only to the extent necessary for the purposes above. If we wish to use it for any other purpose, we will obtain your consent first, unless the law provides otherwise. We do not currently use your personal data to send marketing messages.
  6. If you do not provide certain data, the related features may be affected:
    • If you do not sign in: features that require an account are unavailable.
    • If you do not allow location access: the App cannot calculate distance automatically or draw a route sketch.
    • If you decline AI screenshot fare estimates, usage analytics, or crash reports: only that feature is affected; other features remain available.
    • If you do not allow photo-library access: trip-record images cannot be saved to your photo library, but ending a trip and in-app trip records are unaffected.
  7. Crash reporting: When the App crashes or hits an error, it automatically collects crash and diagnostic data (such as crash time, app and OS versions, device model, and stack traces). We use it only to find problems and improve the App's stability, and it does not include your location, routes, or trip details.

3. Storage and Retention

  1. Data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Data stored on your device is protected by iOS data protection.
  2. Cloud Firestore data is stored in the region configured by the cloud provider. We also use data in the Republic of China (Taiwan).
  3. Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics may be processed in the United States and in other countries or regions where Google or its processors have facilities. Sign-in platforms process data in the regions described in their own privacy policies. AI screenshot fare estimates are processed by the third-party AI provider in the regions where that provider operates.
  4. Account data and settings in Firestore are kept for the life of the account. After you delete your account, we delete this data from production systems within the period needed for that purpose, or as required by law. Backups held by the cloud provider may take additional time to clear.
  5. The original AI screenshot is deleted within the period needed for the estimate. The result is stored on your device. The third-party AI provider keeps data under its own service terms.
  6. Usage data (Analytics) is deleted after the period needed for that purpose, or as required by law. Crash and diagnostic data begins to be deleted after 90 days.
  7. Purchase, subscription, and support records are kept for the period needed for those purposes, or as required by law.
  8. Data stored on your device is under your control. Route tracks are kept for 30 days by default and then deleted automatically; you may change this in "Settings > Data Retention" to not keep tracks, or to 7, 30, or 90 days. Trip summaries are kept until you delete them.
  9. Once a trip-record image is saved to your photo library or shared, it is no longer controlled by the App's retention period or deletion features. We do not receive those images and cannot read or delete them.

  1. We do not use data collected by the App for targeted advertising, and we do not obtain your advertising identifier (IDFA).

  3. Other possible recipients include: when required by law or by a judicial or competent authority following legal process; to prevent a danger to the life, body, freedom, or property of you or another person; or, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or transfer of the business, a successor that continues to process the data under this Policy or a standard no less protective, with prior notice to you.

5. Your Rights and Account Deletion

  1. With respect to your personal data that we hold, you may exercise the following rights, which may not be waived in advance or limited by special agreement:
    • To query or request access.
    • To request a copy.
    • To request supplementation or correction.
    • To request that collection, processing, or use be stopped.
    • To request deletion.
  2. How to exercise these rights: in the App, under "Settings › Account," you may view and edit account and rate settings. You may also write to qaz50805@icloud.com, stating your sign-in method, account email (or Apple relay address), and the right you wish to exercise. To protect your data, we will first verify your identity.
  3. We will decide requests for query, access, or a copy within 15 days, and requests for supplementation, correction, cessation, or deletion within 30 days. Each period may be extended once if necessary, and we will notify you of the reason. Requests for query, access, or a copy are handled under the Personal Data Protection Act.
  4. You may withdraw location or photo-library permission in iPhone "Settings > Privacy & Security." In the App, under "Settings > Privacy," you may turn off usage analytics, crash reports, or AI screenshot fare estimates. You may sign out or unlink a sign-in platform under "Settings › Account."
  5. Trip records, route tracks, and images in your photo library exist only on your device. We do not have a copy and cannot query or delete them for you. You may delete them directly in the App or in your photo library.

Account Deletion

  1. You may delete your account in the App under "Settings › Account › Delete Account." You do not need to write to or contact customer support.
  2. The deletion flow will explain what will and will not be deleted, and may ask you to sign in again to verify your identity. We will ask whether you also want to delete trip records on this device. A confirmation will be shown when deletion is complete.
  3. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a paid plan subscribed through Apple. Renewal charges will still be collected by Apple. Please cancel first on the iPhone subscriptions page or the Apple subscriptions page.
  4. Deletion covers: your account and sign-in data; account, rate, and subscription settings in Cloud Firestore; revocation of the Sign in with Apple token; and records related to AI screenshot fare estimates. Usage and crash data are not linked to your name or email and are deleted automatically under the periods in Chapter 3.
  5. Your account is disabled immediately after you confirm. We delete the data above from production systems within the period needed for that purpose, or keep it as required by law. Cloud backups may take additional time to clear. Data we are legally required to keep is retained only for the legally required period and then deleted.
  6. If you can no longer sign in to the App, you may request deletion by writing to qaz50805@icloud.com.

6. Children

  2. If we learn that we have collected a child's personal data without the required consent, we will delete it promptly.

9. Additional Disclosures (App Store Privacy Details)

  1. Precise location linked to your account: After you sign in, trip records (including the precise GPS route of each trip, times, distance, and fare estimates) are synced to Google Cloud Firestore and linked to your account identifier so you can use and restore them across devices. When you delete your account, this cloud data is deleted as well. Nothing is synced when you use the local test account.

  3. Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics): When the App crashes, it automatically collects crash and diagnostic data (such as crash time, app and OS versions, device model, and stack traces). We use it only to improve stability, and it does not include your location, routes, or trip details.
  4. On-device preferences (UserDefaults): The App uses iOS UserDefaults to store its own preferences on your device (for example, the "auto-save receipt" switch and which release notes you have seen). Only the App reads this data; it is not used to track you and is not sent to third parties.
